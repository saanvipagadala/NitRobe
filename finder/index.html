NitRobe

The gene finder

A logistic regression model is trained on thousands of real copies of nosZ, the one gene on Earth that destroys nitrous oxide. Give the model a bacterium and it reads the whole genome, millions of letters, and finds the gene wherever it sits.

The model runs in the browser. scikit-learn was used to train it. See how it was built →

The genome is cut into slices of 2,000 letters. A new slice starts every 1,000, so they overlap and no gene can fall between 2 of them. Every slice is read forwards and backwards. A slice scoring 0.90 or more is called a hit.
